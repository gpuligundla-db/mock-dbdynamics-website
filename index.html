Straight answers

Business Central questions, answered plainly.

Business Central or F&O — which one is for us?
Under roughly 250 users with standard multi-entity needs: BC, almost always. The honest dividing line is on the F&O page — including when we'd talk you out of F&O.
Do we lose our history when we migrate?
No. Open transactions and master data move into BC; full history stays queryable — either migrated in or kept in a reporting archive you can search from Power BI. What you keep is a design decision we make together in the assessment, not an accident.
How long does an implementation take?
Assessment: two weeks. First entity live: typically 8–14 weeks after that, depending on data and integrations. Phases are fixed-price, so drift is our problem, not yours.
What does it cost?
Licensing is a per-user Microsoft subscription — simple and public. Implementation is priced per phase, fixed, before work starts; you'll have a real number after the free assessment. We don't do open-ended hourly engagements unless you prefer T&M.
What AI is included vs. custom?
Included: Copilot features and Microsoft's BC agents, as they ship. Custom: agents on your specific processes and data — quoted as their own small phases. We'll tell you plainly which is which; most clients start with what's already included.
